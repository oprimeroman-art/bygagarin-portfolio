CONSENT to data processing

A separate consent to send a request through the byGagarin form.

NABERUSHKIN ROMAN OLEGOVICH · NPD (self-employed)

Revision of 18 November 2026.

1. Operator

Consent is given to the individual Roman Olegovich Naberushkin, who applies the special tax regime “Tax on Professional Income” (self-employed), contact address: gagarinfoto64@yandex.ru.

2. What the consent covers

The user freely, of their own will and in their own interest, gives a separate consent to the processing of the personal data that they voluntarily include in a request prepared on the byGagarin website and sent by them independently to the operator by email or via Telegram.

Such data may include name, email address, username or profile name on Telegram, other contact details, the content of the request and correspondence, information about the task, and files voluntarily attached by the user. Do not include passport details, health information, biometric data, bank details, or data of third parties in the request unless there is a separate lawful basis for doing so.

3. Purposes of processing

  • to receive and review the request;
  • to reply to the user and, if necessary, to clarify the circumstances of the task;
  • to prepare a proposal, discuss terms, and conclude a contract.

This consent does not extend to marketing mailings, publication of reviews, distribution of data, or transfer of data for independent marketing by third parties.

4. Actions and methods of processing

The operator may collect, record, systematise, accumulate, store, update, use, block, delete, and destroy the data provided. Processing may be carried out with or without automated means, in email correspondence and in other forms necessary for the stated purposes.

5. Term and withdrawal

The consent is valid until the purposes of processing are achieved, but no longer than 12 months from the date of the last substantive contact, if no contract has been concluded and there are no other lawful grounds for storing the data. If a contract is concluded, the data may continue to be processed to the extent and for the period necessary to perform it and to comply with the requirements of the law.

Consent may be withdrawn by letter to gagarinfoto64@yandex.ru with the subject “Withdrawal of consent to the processing of personal data”. After receiving the withdrawal, the operator stops processing based on consent and destroys the data, unless their further storage is required on another lawful basis, within the time limits set by Article 21 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ.

6. How consent is confirmed on the website

On the contacts page, consent is requested through a separate checkbox that is not pre-ticked and is not combined with confirmation of having read the Privacy Policy. After the checkbox is ticked, the text of the separate consent is added as an independent block to the generated brief. The website does not send the brief automatically: the user opens an email app themselves or copies the text and sends it through the channel of their choice. The operator receives the message and the confirmation text it contains only after the user sends it independently.

If the user has only generated the text in the browser and has not sent it to the operator, the operator receives neither the brief itself nor any information about the checkbox being ticked.
