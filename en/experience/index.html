Work experience

This is my work history, from advertising in Saratov to game products and my own projects. My job titles did not always describe everything I did: I worked with people, processes and all kinds of tasks. Some jobs overlapped, and I have kept the dates as they appear in my CV.

Teleport

October 2017 — June 2019

Head of Advertising and PR

At Teleport I led advertising and PR, and handled websites, SMM, contextual advertising, events and contractors. This was work with people and day-to-day tasks, not just ad accounts.

  • Advertising campaigns from concept to delivery; market and competitor analysis.
  • SMM, search advertising, project websites, events and news hooks.
  • Led and mentored a content manager, a search advertising specialist and freelancers; developed marketing materials.

GagarinFoto

June 2019 — July 2020

Commercial Director

At GagarinFoto I looked after the commercial side of my own photo studio. When a business is small and your own, you are responsible not for a nice sign on the door, but for making sure everything works.

I have kept this period and title as they appear in my CV.

Medical Di-Center

August — November 2019

Head of Marketing

At Di-Center I was responsible for the marketing plan, the team, contractors, budget and reporting. I dealt not only with advertising but also with how all of it was organised internally.

  • Built the marketing plan and budget.
  • Tracked tasks for a team of five and coordinated contractors.
  • Analysed results and prepared reports.

My old Specfinder profile gives the period 2019–2020 and the title “head of advertising department.” My CV says August–November 2019. I am keeping both versions, because old profiles and the timeline do not always match.

Volga Studios

December 2019 — February 2023

CMO / Product PR Manager / Product Manager

At Volga Studios I was responsible for marketing, PR and product. I prepared the Kickstarter, worked with the press, took part in developing the game itself, and handled organisational matters, including office admin duties when the team needed them.

  • Developed strategic and detailed marketing plans to build awareness of the game.
  • Prepared and ran the Kickstarter campaign; worked with the press.

My comment about the game was published by DSOGaming and Twinfinite in April 2021. They listed me as Community Manager, which reflects one real side of my work with the team and its audience.

Apriori Furniture Factory

September 2023 — July 2024

Head of Digital Marketing

At Apriori I led digital marketing: SEO, websites, advertising budgets, contractors, bots, and work with a network of showrooms. As usual, the tasks went well beyond a single advertising channel.

  • Built promotional websites on Tilda and handled SEO for the company’s websites.
  • Consolidated information about the showroom network across Russia and the CIS; worked with reviews.
  • Managed the digital advertising budget, social targeting and tasks for search advertising specialists.
  • Developed and launched Telegram and WhatsApp bots for customer communication; coordinated contractors and controlled quality.

Dates and responsibilities are taken from my CV.

Marketer
