MATERIA Generative Study 001

“Materia” is my experiment with live graphics: form, light and motion are calculated by code directly in the browser.

What happens on screen

The browser calculates the form, light and reflections on the fly. Move the cursor, touch the screen or switch states.

Three states

I made three modes: “Flow” with smooth movement, the more geometric “Structure”, and “Decay”, where the form deforms more strongly.

In the full version I added pause, fullscreen mode and keys 1, 2 and 3 to switch states. On this page you can also change the mode, give the scene an impulse and pause it.

Procedural sound

The sound is synthesised directly in the browser and is off by default. It can be turned on inside “Materia”; on the portfolio pages nothing plays on its own.

How it's used here

I put a lightweight version of “Materia” on the home page and in the tools. It lives right among the site's elements, responds to movement and changes state. The text and navigation do not depend on it.

If reduced motion is enabled, the form freezes. If the browser doesn't support WebGL, a static image stays in place, and the site remains usable.
