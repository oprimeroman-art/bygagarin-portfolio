My role

From December 2019 to February 2023 I worked at Volga Studios in Saratov. On paper I was CMO, product PR manager and Product Manager. In practice I dealt not only with promotion but with the product itself. When something in a small studio needed solving, I could be both organiser and office manager. Job titles only describe part of what I did.

  • I built the strategy and detailed promotion plans for game products.
  • I prepared and ran the Kickstarter crowdfunding campaign.
  • I communicated with the game press, prepared materials and presented the game to audiences.

This was work not only with advertising but with the game itself. My involvement is visible in publications from those years. I left the full timeline in my biography.

Full timeline

Campaign

We launched the Kickstarter on 4 November 2020; it was scheduled to end on 3 January 2021. Kicktraq still holds the figures: 229 backers, $32 739 in pledged contributions against a goal of $100 000 — about 32.7% of the planned amount.

The campaign was later cancelled. We did not reach the goal. But over the course of the work we found 229 backers, received attention from the international press, showed the game to audiences and took plenty of hard knocks. For me this is part of real product work, not a line on a résumé.

About the project

At Volga Studios we made The Emperor’s Own, a story-driven adventure with RPG elements. An alternate early 19th-century empire, a secret intelligence officer, decisions with consequences for history. I was inside the work on the game product, not just someone who was supposed to advertise it.

On 4 November 2020 we released The Emperor’s Own: Alpha Teaser on Steam for free. It still lists Volga Studios as developer and publisher. You can open the page and see what we showed players.
