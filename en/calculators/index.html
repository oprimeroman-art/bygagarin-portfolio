CALCULATORS for work.

I’ve collected here the things I constantly need to calculate myself: advertising, profit, payback, sales, customers and project economics. Enter the numbers and see the result right away. No registration, no data sent to a server.

All calculations run directly in your browser. Empty denominators are shown as a dash, not as zero or infinity. Formulas for each calculation are shown openly. Forecasts are scenarios under the stated conditions.

01 / 16

Advertising funnel

CTR, CPM, CPC, CPL, CAC, conversions and ROAS from a single set of numbers.

Formula: CTR = clicks / impressions × 100%; CPL = ad budget / leads; CAC = budget / sales; ROAS = revenue / budget.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

02 / 16

ROI — return on investment

How much the project earned or lost after all the listed expenses.

Formula: ROI = (revenue − all costs) / all costs × 100%.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

03 / 16

ROMI — return on marketing investment

Accounts for sales margin: I don’t confuse revenue with earned money.

Formula: ROMI = (revenue × margin − marketing costs) / marketing costs × 100%.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

04 / 16

Profit and profitability

What is left after variable, fixed and marketing costs.

Formula: Profit = revenue − variable − fixed − marketing costs. Profitability = profit / revenue × 100%.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

05 / 16

Margin and markup

I check the price of a product or service and don’t confuse margin with markup.

Formula: Margin = (price − cost price) / price × 100%; markup = (price − cost price) / cost price × 100%.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

06 / 16

Break-even point

How many units must be sold to cover fixed costs.

Formula: Units = round up [fixed costs / (price − variable cost per unit)].

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

07 / 16

Revenue for target profit

Reverse calculation: how much must be earned to cover costs and reach the goal.

Formula: Revenue = (fixed costs + desired profit) / contribution margin ratio.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

08 / 16

Discount and its consequences

How much will need to be sold after a discount to keep the same gross profit.

Formula: New price = price × (1 − discount); required volume = old gross profit / new profit per unit.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

09 / 16

LTV — customer value

A simple model of revenue and gross profit over the entire customer relationship.

Formula: LTV by gross profit = average check × purchases per year × years of relationship × margin.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

10 / 16

LTV to CAC ratio

I compare the gross profit from a customer with the cost of acquiring them.

Formula: LTV / CAC = customer gross profit over the period / cost of acquiring them.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

11 / 16

ARPU and ARPPU

Average revenue per user and per paying user.

Formulas: ARPU = revenue / active users; ARPPU = revenue / paying users.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

12 / 16

Customer churn

What share of the client base left during the period and how many remain.

Formula: Churn = clients lost / clients at the start of the period × 100%.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

13 / 16

CAC payback period

How many months of gross profit from a client it takes to cover the cost of acquiring them.

Formula: Months = CAC / (monthly revenue per client × margin).

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

14 / 16

Media plan by CPM

Budget for the given impressions and a scenario forecast of clicks and leads.

Formulas: Budget = impressions / 1000 × CPM; clicks = impressions × CTR; leads = clicks × conversion rate.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

15 / 16

How many clicks and how much money

A plan worked backwards from a sales target, through conversion rates and CPC.

Formula: Clicks = sales / (click-to-lead conversion × lead-to-sale conversion); budget = clicks × CPC.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.

Savings from automation

Time is money too: what the process costs and when automation pays for itself.

Formula: Monthly savings = hours × cost per hour; payback period = implementation cost / monthly savings.

For the interactive calculation, enable JavaScript. The formula is shown above.
