POLICY personal data

What data the website processes, why, where it arises and how the user can manage it.

NABERUSHKIN ROMAN OLEGOVICH · NPD (self-employed)

Revision of 18 November 2026.

1. Operator and scope

Personal data operator: Naberushkin Roman Olegovich, an individual applying the special tax regime “Tax on Professional Income” (self-employed). Contact for questions about the processing of personal data: gagarinfoto64@yandex.ru.

This policy applies to the byGagarin website and to messages that the user chooses to send to the operator by email or via Telegram. It describes how the website actually works as of the revision date and does not mean that the website automatically receives or stores every form submission.

2. What data is processed

  • Message text: the selected direction, task description, desired outcome, and any details the user includes in the message.
  • Sender data: name and email address shown when a message is received; profile name and Telegram username if the message is sent via messenger; other contact details voluntarily provided in the message.
  • Correspondence and attachments: the content of replies and files that the user has sent to the operator.
  • Hosting technical data: when visiting GitHub Pages, the hosting service itself may log the visitor's IP address for security purposes. According to GitHub's documentation, such IP addresses are recorded and stored by GitHub; the site operator does not receive these logs through the form or the site interface. More details: GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub privacy statement.

The operator does not request passport details, health information, biometric data, bank details, or special categories of personal data through the form. Do not include such information in the task description. Do not send other people's data without a lawful basis.

3. How the form works

When you click Send request, the selected direction, task description, desired outcome, contact details and consent confirmation are sent to the form handler on the website hosting in Russia and emailed to the operator at gagarinfoto64@yandex.ru. An email app or messenger is not required. Fields are not transmitted while you type.

The form does not create a public database of requests. Messages are stored in the operator’s mailbox. A temporary hash of the visitor’s IP address and request times are used to limit spam; form contents are not saved in that temporary file.

4. Purposes and legal grounds for processing

  • Replying to a message, clarifying the task, preparing a proposal, and negotiating a contract. Basis: actions taken at the request of the data subject to conclude a contract and, after conclusion, performance of the contract (clause 5, part 1, article 6 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ), as well as the user's separate consent where it is requested through the form.
  • Fulfilling obligations established by law. Basis: the relevant provisions of the legislation of the Russian Federation, including when drawing up and performing a contract and keeping mandatory records.
  • Protecting the operator's rights and legitimate interests. Processing correspondence and information related to a message may be necessary to confirm agreements and protect rights within the limits permitted by law.

The data is not used for automated decisions that produce legal consequences for the user, and is not used for marketing mailings without a separate legal basis.

5. Processing activities and services used

After a message is received, the operator may collect, review, organise, clarify, use, store, restrict, and delete the personal data it contains, only to the extent necessary for the stated purposes.

  • Email: messages are accepted at the address gagarinfoto64@yandex.ru. Delivery and storage of email correspondence also depend on the email services of the sender and recipient, which follow their own rules.
  • Telegram: if the user chooses this channel, messages pass through the Telegram service. Telegram processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy. The operator does not control this service's infrastructure or its independent processing of data.
  • GitHub Pages: is used to host the site. Technical security logs of the hosting service are processed by GitHub under its own rules.

The operator does not sell personal data, pass it to advertising networks, or instruct third parties to send advertising. Disclosure to state authorities is possible only in cases and in the manner provided by law.

6. Cookies and local storage

As of the revision date, the byGagarin site code does not use cookies, web analytics systems, advertising pixels, or behavioural tracking tools. For this reason, no separate consent banner for advertising or analytics cookies is set on the site.

To remember the interface accessibility setting, namely the choice of reduced motion, the site uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) with the key gagarin-reduced-motion. This value is stored in the user's browser, is not sent to the operator, and is not used to identify the visitor. It can be deleted in the browser's site data settings.

This description does not override the independent processing of technical data by the hosting provider, described in section 2.

7. Retention periods

If a message does not lead to a contract, the correspondence and related data are kept for no longer than 12 months from the date of the last substantive contact, after which they are deleted unless another legal basis for further storage exists. If a contract is concluded, documents and information are kept for as long as necessary to perform the contract, meet legal requirements, and protect the parties' rights.

GitHub Pages technical logs are stored by GitHub itself in accordance with its own rules and retention periods; the site operator does not manage these logs.

8. User rights and requests

The user may request information about the processing of their personal data, demand its correction, restriction, or destruction where legally permitted grounds exist, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To do so, send an email to gagarinfoto64@yandex.ru with the subject “Personal data”. Indicate which message the request relates to so the operator can locate the relevant correspondence. Withdrawing consent does not stop processing if another legal basis for it remains. The time limits for ending processing and destroying data are set by article 21 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ.

9. Data protection

The operator takes necessary and sufficient organisational and technical measures to protect received data from unlawful access, alteration, disclosure, restriction, copying, and destruction. Access to correspondence is limited to the person handling the relevant request. Data must not be used for purposes incompatible with those for which it was provided.

10. Changes to this policy

The current version is published on this page. If the form changes, analytics are added, new services are introduced, requests are stored on a server, or data is handled in any other new way, the policy must be updated before that processing begins.

Operator contact: Naberushkin Roman Olegovich · gagarinfoto64@yandex.ru · @gagarin64ru.
