01 / Task

First, understand
what is holding back growth.

I don't think marketing work is just setting up ads. I'm interested in the whole system: the product itself, the people it's made for, the offer, customer acquisition, and what happens after the first contact.

01
Audit · Strategy · Positioning

Unclear who the product is for and why it's needed

I analyse the audience, competitors, offer and customer journey. I help formulate positioning, set priorities and build a realistic promotion plan.

  • Audit of marketing, website and acquisition channels
  • Research of audience and competitive environment
  • Positioning, UVP and marketing strategy
  • Media plan, budget and measurable KPIs
Result: a clear picture of the current situation, priorities and an action plan.
02
Internet marketing · SEO · Advertising

You need enquiries, not just traffic

I work with search promotion, advertising and content. I look not only at clicks, but also at what happens after the visit: an enquiry, a request, a purchase or a bounce.

  • SEO audit and organic traffic growth
  • Contextual and targeted advertising
  • SMM, content plan and community work
  • Funnel, conversion and ad budget analytics
Result: linking acquisition channels, the website and analytics with clear performance criteria.
03
PR · Media · Communications

The project should be known and properly understood

I help build a public image for a product: from shaping its story and press materials to making contact with editors, bloggers and relevant communities.

  • PR strategy and communications plan
  • Working with journalists, bloggers and industry media
  • Press releases, articles, interviews and content
  • Promoting launches and independent projects
Result: consistent communication and materials that explain the value of the product.
04
Product · Games · Independent development

A product needs to reach the market

A separate area of my work is marketing for games and digital products. I take into account the specifics of indie development, small teams, limited budgets and the long cycle of building a product.

  • Positioning and presentation of a game product
  • Launch plan, audience and promotion channels
  • Working with game media and communities
  • Preparing campaigns and materials for crowdfunding
Result: a promotion plan that matches the development stage and the team’s real resources.

02 / Practice

Experience you can
verify.

Marketing and PR have been my profession for many years. I have worked in them at Saratov companies, a game studio and my own projects. Here I show not a list of fine words, but what I have actually worked on.

01
Volga Studios · 2019–2023

CMO / PR / Product

At Volga Studios I was responsible for marketing and PR and took part in developing the game The Emperor’s Own itself. I prepared the Kickstarter, dealt with the press and handled organisational matters too. The campaign reached 229 backers and $32,739 in pledged contributions against a goal of $100,000, and was then cancelled. It was not a successful raise, but it was serious hands-on experience.

Advertising · Marketing · Digital

Work with companies and teams

At Teleport I ran advertising and PR, at Di-center I led marketing, and at Apriori I headed the digital direction. At Volga Studios I worked on marketing, product and press at the same time. no magic button.
