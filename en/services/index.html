01 / What challenges

Situations, not
price list.

My work doesn’t stop at setting up ads or writing code. I can look at a task from a marketing standpoint, help with the product, build a website or automate a process. For projects in other cities, I work remotely.

01
Internet marketing · SEO · PR

Clients aren't coming in, and ads don't pay off

I look at the offer, the audience, the website and the ads. I check where people come from and at which step they drop off. Then we decide what to fix.

  • Marketing and acquisition channel audit
  • Positioning, strategy and media plan
  • SEO, content, PR and community work
  • Funnel analytics and clear reporting
Deliverables: a map of problems, priorities, an action plan and, if needed, setup and management of the chosen channels.

02
Product · Research · Launch

I have an idea, but it's unclear how to turn it into a product

I help define who the product is for, what problem it solves, and what really needs to be done in the first working version. From concept to a testable prototype and launch.

  • Analysis of the audience, competitors and usage scenarios
  • Product structure and user flows
  • Feature prioritisation and technical specification
  • Prototype, launch and development plan
Deliverables: an agreed concept, a clear scope of work, a prototype or a working product.

03
Website development · Tools · Integrations

You need a website or tool that actually works

I build websites, catalogues and small applications. Where a ready-made solution fits, I use it. Where custom code is needed, I build it and check it with AI. Working results matter more to me than complexity for its own sake.

  • Structure, content, interface and SEO foundation
  • Catalogues, search, forms and user scenarios
  • Integrations, APIs and automation of repetitive operations
  • WordPress customisation, small plugins and integrations as needed
  • Publication, testing and handover
Deliverables: a published website, application or agreed tool. I also develop and set up 360° scenes built with Panorama Studio.

04
Processes · Content · Teams

The work relies on manual tasks and constant reminders

I look at the whole process: who does what, where information gets lost, which operations repeat, and where automation is genuinely worth the complexity.

  • Process mapping and division of responsibilities
  • Editorial and production pipelines
  • Service integrations, bots and checkpoints
  • Documentation and result review
Deliverables: a simpler workflow, instructions and tools that work.

05
Non-standard and mixed tasks

It's not even clear yet how to name the problem

We don't start with choosing a service, but with a conversation about the situation. We separate symptoms from causes, define the constraints, and decide what you can do yourself and where you need specialists.

  • Initial review and task definition
  • Research of options and risks
  • Building a plan and a team if needed
  • Implementation control and result review
Deliverables: a clear problem statement and the next sensible step, even if it doesn't turn into a joint project.
