At Teleport I worked on advertising campaigns, websites, SMM, contextual advertising and events. I managed the work of a content manager, a Direct specialist and freelancers. I also took on tasks that did not fit within the job title.

June 2019 — July 2020

“GagarinFoto” · Commercial Director

At GagarinFoto I ran my own photo services studio. I was responsible for commercial work and the day-to-day running of the business. In a role like that, you can't simply say “that's not my job.”

Di-Center Medical · Marketing Lead

At Di-Center I was responsible for the marketing plan, a team of five, contractors, the budget, analytics and reporting. The job meant not only coming up with promotion, but also organising people and the work itself.

Specfinder lists the period as 2019–2020, while my résumé says August–November 2019. The old records don't fully match, so I've kept both dates.
